# ✅ V1 Features (Summary)

🇬🇧 **English** | [🇪🇸 Español](./FeaturesV1.es.md)

---

Short list, one line per capability, of what the V1 is going to do per the [full specification](../Specification/SpecificationV1.es.md) (Spanish only, since it's the authoritative reference Claude Code works from). This is the "quick review" version; each point's detail lives in that document.

## Recurring expenses

1. Create a recurring expense (subscription, utility, insurance, tax, fee, loan, or other).
2. Edit an expense.
3. Pause, cancel, or reactivate an expense, without losing its history.
4. Delete an expense (with its charges and history).
5. Set how often it's billed (every N days, weeks, months, or years, or only in certain months).
6. Choose whether the amount is fixed, variable, or approximate.
7. Log an actual charge by hand.
8. Delete a logged charge.
9. Preview an expense before saving it ("here's how it'll look").
10. Note contract details: commitment period, promotional price, free trial, and cancellation notice period.

## Deadlines and pricing

11. See the deadline to cancel, calculated from the notice period.
12. See an expense's price-increase history and how much you've paid in total.

## Summary and forecast

13. See the summary: monthly total, yearly total, active expenses, next 30 days, and how much to set aside each month.
14. See spending grouped by type (ring chart).
15. See what's coming up soon: cancellation deadlines, end of promotion, end of free trial, and end of commitment.
16. See the 90-day forecast, month by month.
17. See how much will be charged before your next payday and between one payday and the next.
18. See how much you should have saved for expenses that aren't monthly (the "savings bucket").
19. Configure your payday and whether you get 12 or 14 paychecks a year.

## Bank statement import

20. Import your bank statement (Excel or CSV) and have the app detect recurring charges.
21. Review and correct the detected columns from the statement before confirming.
22. Confirm the import and create all detected expenses at once.

## Service catalog

23. Search the Spanish service catalog (around 33 services) to auto-fill an expense.

## Calendar, backups and demo mode

24. Download a calendar (ICS) with charges and deadlines.
25. Download a full backup of your data (JSON).
26. Restore a backup (replaces all data).
27. Import data from Wallos.
28. Turn on a demo mode with sample data, and leave it without a trace.
